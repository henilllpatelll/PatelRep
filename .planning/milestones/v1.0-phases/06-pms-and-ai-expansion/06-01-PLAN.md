<objective>
Fix the two credit-accounting defects research found in the already-shipped AI copilot, proving each with a regression test first (RED) then landing the fix (GREEN):

1. **Flat credit cost (CLAUDE.md A3 violation).** `middleware/credits.py::check_and_deduct_credits()` charges a flat per-interaction-type amount from `CREDIT_COSTS` (e.g. `task_creation: 1.0`, `sop_query: 2.0`) completely disconnected from the real `prompt_tokens`/`completion_tokens` the LLM call consumed. Real usage IS already returned by every parser and IS written to `ai_interactions`, but the billable credit amount never derives from it. This is the mechanism the Stripe monthly true-up depends on.
2. **Double `ai_interactions` write on the sop_query path.** `services/ai/sop_rag.py::query_sop()` writes its own `ai_interactions` row (hardcoded `credits_charged=2.0`), AND `routers/ai_copilot.py::copilot_chat()` writes a second row after `check_and_deduct_credits()`. Every SOP question through the copilot double-writes the audit trail, corrupting `ai_interactions` aggregates (the GM-facing "AI credits used (7d)" stat in `services/ai/insights.py`).

Purpose: Bring the AI credit ledger and audit trail to Phase 1–5 trust standard (D-02/D-06). Fix-in-place per D-05 — no deferral.
Output: A token-derived credit computation, a single audit-log owner for SOP queries, and a new `test_ai_copilot_credits.py` proving both.
</objective>
